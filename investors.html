Across the investment cycle

Three points where I help.

01 / Before you buy

Due diligence.

I've been through three sales on the seller's side, so I've seen the questions buyers ask and where the answers tend to get stuck. When I look at a company you're thinking of buying, I check its data, technology, security and team against what you plan to do with it. I also look at where AI could add value once you own it. That gives you a view of the upside as well as the risks before you commit.

02 / While you own it

Value creation.

AI only adds value when it fits the way a company works. I look at how a change would sit within the business. That means which systems it touches, and how it changes people's day-to-day work. I don't fix one thing in isolation. I work across the company so the changes add up to the targets you've set.

03 / Before you sell

Exit.

Buyers look closely at a company's data, its use of AI, its security and how it runs day to day. I help get those in good shape well before the sale starts. Then the answers are ready when the questions come, and the process tends to go more smoothly.
