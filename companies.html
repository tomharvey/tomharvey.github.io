How I work

Hands-on, with your whole team.

AI isn't only for engineers. Some of the biggest gains I've seen came from finance, people and marketing teams building tools for their own work. At one company, the people team built themselves a successful sourcing tool that ran around the clock and helped make multiple hires.

People first

I learn the business quickly.

I start with one-to-ones with the people who run the business, in every team, not only the technical ones. My AI evaluation framework shows me who will pick things up fast and who needs some coaching first. Seniority doesn't tell me that. I fit the support to each person, so the whole team moves faster.

Champions, then the company

I start with a small group.

I begin with a small group of champions. They come from wherever the energy is, and that's finance as often as engineering. Once their results show, the rest of the company tends to follow.

Hands-on builder

I build the technical parts.

Some of the work still needs an engineer's care. The data foundations, and the connectors that put AI across your systems, need technical best practice. I design and build that myself, or with your technical staff.

Checkable by design

Never “AI did it”.

Teams learn to build AI work they can check. A finance team builds a spreadsheet with formulas they can review, not numbers pasted in. The evidence behind a result matters as much as the result.
