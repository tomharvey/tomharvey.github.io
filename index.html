What I believe

A few things I've learned.

The real intelligence is in the people and tools around the model.

The leading models already handle most of what a business asks of them. The difference comes from the tools and checks built around them. They keep a person in the driving seat, with AI as the tool.

AI has read every book, but not your operating manual.

AI doesn't know your goals, your customers or what “good” means in your business. Most of the work is teaching AI those things.

Data was always the new oil. Most companies kept it in the ground.

AI is the first tool that lets everyone in a company use their data. Data-heavy companies gain the most, as long as the data is in good shape. Getting it there is where I start.

If you can't check it, you can't trust it.

An insight is only as good as the evidence behind it. I build AI that shows its sources and every step it took. That evidence chain matters as much as the answer.

How I see it

AI is the tool.
People make it work.

AI changes how fast a business can grow and improve. The gains are biggest in technical domains with a lot of data, like energy and insurance. The value of AI compounds over time. So the companies that adopt it early gain a lead that is hard to close.

But that lead depends on people. A team that knows how to use AI gets more done, and does better work. I work with each person, at every level, to learn how they work and what they need. Then I help them build AI into that work. Teams that own the tools reshape their processes and transform your company.
